# Learn.Trimble Guide

Learn.Trimble serves as the central platform for all training courses available in Trimble SEA. This guide outlines the standard workflow for trainers and participants to ensure smooth course delivery and certification.

&rarr; **[Access the Learn.Trimble Platform](https://learn.trimble.com/learn/signin)**

---

<div style="background-color: #f4f7f9; border-left: 8px solid #005a8c; padding: 10px 20px; margin-top: 30px; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <h2 style="margin: 0; color: #005a8c; font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 100; border-bottom: none; padding: 0;">Platform Workflow</h2>
</div>


The training process on Learn.Trimble follows these five key stages:

1. **Course Availability:** General course structures have been pre-created on the platform for all available training topics.
2. **Session Creation:** Trainers are required to create specific **Sessions** under the relevant course for each training batch.
3. **Participant Enrollment:** Participants must be invited to enroll in the specific session created for their training dates.
4. **Quiz Completion:** After attending the session, participants are required to complete an online quiz to validate their learning.
5. **Credential Issuance:** Upon successful completion of the quiz, a digital credential (certificate) is automatically issued to the participant.
