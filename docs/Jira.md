# Writing Effective Jira Issues

The goal of a Jira issue is to get a bug fixed or a feature added. Follow these guidelines to write effective descriptions that accelerate the resolution process.

!!!info "Why Quality Matters:" 
	Creating good issue reports can be time-consuming, but it is a critical investment. Issues that lack the information highlighted in these guides are likely to be sent back to the reporter or may not be considered for development.
