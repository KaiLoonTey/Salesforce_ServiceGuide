# Log a Call or Event

Recording interactions is essential for case history and tracking. Learn how to correctly log "meaningful interactions" on a case.

---

## Log a Call

Use the <span style="color: #0063A3;">**Log a Call**</span> feature when a phone call with the customer is required to discuss the case. This is used to document both inbound and outbound calls.

1. On the case page, navigate to the <span style="color: #0063A3;">**Feed & Actions**</span> tab.
2. Click the <span style="color: #0063A3;">**Log a Call**</span> action.
3. Enter a <span style="color: #0063A3;">**Subject**</span> for the call and detailed notes in the <span style="color: #0063A3;">**Comments**</span> section.
4. Ensure the <span style="color: #0063A3;">**Correct Contact**</span> is associated with the call log.
5. Click <span style="color: #0063A3;">**Save**</span>.

## Create a New Event

Use the <span style="color: #0063A3;">**New Event**</span> feature to schedule and record activities like a <span style="color: #0063A3;">**TeamViewer/Zoom session**</span> or an <span style="color: #0063A3;">**online consultation**</span>. Events have a defined start and end time.

1. On the case page, navigate to the <span style="color: #0063A3;">**Feed & Actions**</span> tab.
2. Click the <span style="color: #0063A3;">**New Event**</span> action.
3. Fill in the <span style="color: #0063A3;">**Subject**</span>, <span style="color: #0063A3;">**Description**</span>, and set the <span style="color: #0063A3;">**Start/End Date and Time**</span>.
4. Add any relevant <span style="color: #0063A3;">**Attendees**</span> (internal staff and customer contacts).
5. Click <span style="color: #0063A3;">**Save**</span>.

![New Event Interface](assets/SF_CreateEvent.png){width="50%"}

---

!!! info "Why Log Activities?"
    Logging calls and events provides a complete timeline of interactions, helps other team members understand case progress, and contributes to key support metrics.
