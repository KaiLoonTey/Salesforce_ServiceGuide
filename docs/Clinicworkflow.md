# Customer Clinic Workflow

Standard operating procedure for arranging clinic sessions, evaluating ILT requirements, and aligning with the Sales team.

---

!!!note "Session Limits & Policies"

	*   **Limit (Same Issue):** A maximum of 4 sessions total are permitted for the exact same issue before triggering an escalation to Sales.
	*   **Limit (Different Issues):** A maximum of 4 consecutive sessions in a row are permitted for different issues before evaluating for further training.
	*   **Tracking (Remarks Log):** Every session's specific details and issues must be recorded in the remarks column on the shared Google Sheet.
	*   **Analysis (Datalocker Studio):** Verify the total number of sessions per company strictly using the centralized Datalocker Studio dashboard.

!!! info "Workflow Objective"
    By closely monitoring session frequency (reaching the 4-session threshold) and categorizing the nature of inquiries, we can accurately determine if a client genuinely requires comprehensive Instructor-Led Training (ILT). This allows us to transition from ad-hoc support to providing proactive, structured educational solutions via the Sales team.

---

## Execution & Verification

!!! warning "Mandatory Sales Alignment"
    When an internal notification is triggered after 4 sessions, you must CC the Sales Manager on the email containing the recommended training courses to ensure complete visibility.

1. **Clinic Session Arrangement:** Have the customer book a Clinic session. Ask them to consolidate their questions in advance. Limit the session to exactly 1 hour and record it for their future reference. Add the issue to the remarks column. 
&rarr; **[Open GSheet Tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1BoSCP3rqUiTEcGh4q2cEJN2tDRnq02NRAb39mi6LKwU/edit?gid=1595896300#gid=1595896300)**

2. **Verification:** 
Use the Datalocker Studio dashboard to verify the current number of sessions attended by the specific company.

3. **Evaluation:** 
Once the customer reaches the 4-session limit (either 4 consecutive times for different issues, or 4 times total for the same issue), formally evaluate if they genuinely require Instructor-Led Training (ILT).

4. **Internal Notification:** 
If ILT is deemed necessary, send an internal email officially notifying the Sales team. Include recommended training courses so Sales can proactively offer them to the customer.
