    Emergency License Guidelines

    A quick-reference guide to the eligibility, approval and usage rules for Emergency licenses issued by Trimble Channel Partners and Area Offices.

    Policy Overview

    Trimble grants Channel Partners and Area Offices Emergency licenses to provide temporary software access for customers whose production licenses are unavailable.

    • Purpose-restricted: Emergency licenses may be used only to restore temporary access while production licenses are unavailable.
    • Temporary by design: Support personnel must remove the Emergency license immediately once production access is restored.
    • Existing agreements apply: Users remain bound by their existing software agreements throughout the emergency access period.
    • Support entitlement: Access to Emergency licenses is part of the Support Services provided to customers whose production licenses have an active maintenance contract or an active subscription agreement.

    Maximum Duration

    Emergency access is limited to a maximum of 5 days. Access must be removed as soon as the production licenses have been recovered, even if this happens before the 5 days are up.

    Approval Workflow

    Execution Steps

    1. Confirm the Technical Failure: Verify that a genuine technical failure is preventing the customer from accessing their legitimate production licenses.
    2. Open a Salesforce Support Case: Make sure the outage is documented under an open, validated Salesforce support case that describes the customer's loss of access.
    3. Check Entitlement: Confirm that the affected production licenses are covered by an active maintenance contract or an active subscription agreement.
    4. Obtain Commercial Approval: Make sure written commercial approval from the Renewals team is on file before granting access.
    5. Assign the License (Customer Support only): Customer Support issues the Emergency license for a maximum of 5 days.
    6. Remove Access: Once the production licenses are recovered, remove the Emergency license immediately.

    Eligibility at a Glance

    ✅ Permitted ❌ Not Permitted
    Verified technical failure preventing access to legitimate production licenses Interim bridging during sales fulfilment or order provisioning delays
    Outage documented under an open, validated Salesforce support case Temporary access for customers with unpaid invoices or credit holds
    Written commercial approval from the Renewals team on file Uncontrolled workarounds for recurring license borrowing errors
    Customer has an active maintenance contract or subscription Software evaluations, customer trials or commercial betas
    Duration of 5 days or less Internal use or functional testing by Trimble employees or channel personnel
    Duration longer than 5 days

    Compliance Reminder

    Emergency licenses are not a substitute for order fulfilment, trials or internal testing. Any request outside the permitted scenarios above must be declined and sent through the appropriate commercial or provisioning channel.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Who can assign an Emergency license?

    Only Customer Support can assign Emergency licenses.

    Important Note: An Emergency license can only be granted based on a support ticket detailing the customer's loss of access to their production licenses.

    In what situations can we use an Emergency license?

    An Emergency license may be issued only when all of the following conditions are met:

    • A verified technical failure is preventing access to legitimate production licenses.
    • The outage is documented under an open, validated Salesforce support case.
    • Written commercial approval from the Renewals team is on file.
    • The access lasts no longer than 5 days. Access must be removed once the licenses have been recovered.
    What is an Emergency license NOT allowed to be used for?

    Emergency licenses must not be used for:

    • Interim bridging during sales fulfilment or order provisioning delays.
    • Temporary software access for customers with unpaid invoices or credit holds.
    • Uncontrolled workarounds for recurring license borrowing errors.
    • Software evaluations, customer trials or commercial betas.
    • Internal use or functional testing by Trimble employees or channel personnel.
    • Any duration longer than 5 days.
    Which customers are entitled to an Emergency license?

    Access to Emergency licenses is part of the Support Services provided to customers whose production licenses have an active maintenance contract or an active subscription agreement.

    What is the maximum duration of an Emergency license?

    Emergency access is limited to 5 days. Support personnel must remove the license immediately once production access is restored, even if this happens before the 5 days are up.

    Do the customer's existing software agreements still apply?

    Yes. Users remain bound by their existing software agreements while using an Emergency license.

    Can an Emergency license cover a delay in order processing or license delivery?

    No. Interim bridging during sales fulfilment or order provisioning delays is explicitly not permitted. Delivery issues should be raised with the relevant Order Admin team instead.
